# Tetanus — 1-page notes

- **Organism:** *Clostridium tetani* — Gram-positive, obligate anaerobe, spore-forming rod; round terminal spore = **drumstick appearance**.
- **Habitat:** soil, dust, animal (esp. horse) faeces; manure-treated soil is rich in spores.
- **Spores:** resist heat and usual antiseptics; may survive autoclaving at 121 °C for 10–15 min (CDC Pink Book).
- **Entry:** deep/puncture wounds, burns, crush injuries, animal bites, dirty needles; low O2 lets spores germinate.
- **Toxins:** tetanospasmin (neurotoxin) + tetanolysin. Minimum human lethal dose ≈ 2.5 ng/kg (≈175 ng for 70 kg).
- **Mechanism:** retrograde axonal transport to the CNS → blocks release of inhibitory transmitters (GABA, glycine) → unopposed contraction and spasms.
- **Incubation:** 3–21 days (avg 8). Further from the CNS = longer; shorter = more severe.
- **Clinical:** trismus (lockjaw) first → neck stiffness, dysphagia, abdominal rigidity; risus sardonicus; opisthotonus; spasms triggered by stimuli; can fracture spine or long bones; consciousness usually preserved.
- **Treatment:** HTIG neutralises only *unbound* toxin; wound care, supportive/ICU care. ~1 in 10 cases fatal (CDC).
- **No immunity after disease** → vaccinate after recovery.
- **Prevention:** toxoid vaccine. India: TT replaced by **Td**. Adults: booster every 10 years; for deep or dirty wounds, if the last dose was over 5 years ago or is unknown, see a doctor.
- **India:** maternal & neonatal tetanus elimination validated in 2015.
